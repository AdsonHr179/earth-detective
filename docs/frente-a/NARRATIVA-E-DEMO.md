## Short contribution narrative (ED29)

Earth Detective helps students and curious members of the public investigate environmental observations at a selected location. Users choose a variable, point and period, inspect a monthly time series, and see the source, acquisition time, coverage and limits beside the reported change. The initial case uses NASA POWER temperature derived from MERRA-2 at one grid cell in Ceará. Saved analyses can be reopened and exported by ID. The interface distinguishes descriptive estimates from validated inference and flags legacy methods. A short record at one cell cannot establish a statewide climate trend, a cause or a forecast.
