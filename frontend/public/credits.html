Credits and AI use

Environmental data and boundaries

  • NASA POWER monthly point API: the initial temperature case uses MERRA-2-derived observations. Source, method, dates and acquisition time accompany each result. This does not imply NASA endorsement.
  • Optional NASA archive products and Meteomatics are available only when the service confirms access. A configured entry does not establish availability, rights or scientific validation.
  • IBGE geographic boundaries provide location context. Boundaries do not turn a grid-cell observation into a state average.

NASA challenge learning resource

The listed SeaWiFS time-series study (Acker and colleagues, 2009) provides an example of why measured values require context before broader interpretation. Its coastal chlorophyll case is used in our interpretation note, not as validation of temperature inference. NASA POWER supplies the temperature observations. POWER is not directly named in the challenge Resources list; final acceptance of this combination remains for the organizers.

Limits

One cell and a short period cannot establish a statewide climate trend, a cause or a validated forecast. The interface marks descriptive or legacy inference explicitly. Resource-list compliance, final scientific acceptance, public operation and official submission must be verified separately by the team.
